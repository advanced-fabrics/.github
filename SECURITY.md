# Security Policy

Do not report vulnerabilities in public issues. Send reports to security@advfab.org with affected versions, reproduction details and potential impact. Maintainers will acknowledge a report within three business days and coordinate disclosure after a fix is available.

Supported releases and published security advisories are listed at https://docs.advfab.org/security/. Release artifacts are expected to include signatures, provenance and SBOM attestations; consumers must verify the immutable digest rather than relying only on a tag.
