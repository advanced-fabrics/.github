# Governance

Advanced Fabrics begins with a Bootstrap Lead model. The Bootstrap Lead appoints maintainers using documented contribution evidence and records project decisions publicly.

Governance transitions to a Maintainer Council after all of the following are true: at least three active maintainers, representation from at least two independent employers, and two consecutive public releases. The transition is ratified through an AFEP; thereafter ordinary decisions use a simple majority and governance changes require two-thirds approval.

RE8CH is the initiating sponsor but receives no permanent veto, trademark exception or private API authority. Project status must never imply CNCF membership before formal acceptance.
